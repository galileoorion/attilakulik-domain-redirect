Appendix C — Master Checklist

Continue in Reference

Every chapter ends with a revision checklist, a set of questions to put to a draft once the chapter’s tests have been understood. This appendix gathers the eighteen checklists in one place and in one order, the order of the revision ladder set out in The Structural Edit (Chapter 18): first what the book promises, then how it is allocated and arranged, then its shape, then its joins, and its scenes and their materials last. A writer who works down the list in this order makes each decision after the decisions it rests on, so that a later repair does not undo an earlier one.

The questions are copied from the chapters without change, under each chapter’s own headings, and the chapter title above each set links to the chapter. Where a question uses a term, the chapter defines it, and the glossary gives it too. A question that cannot be answered is not a failure in itself: it usually means the decision it asks about has not yet been made, and the chapter says how to make it.

Rung 4: the joins

The fourth rung asks how each unit meets the next: where the chapters break, how gaps are crossed, how the past is entered and left, and what the opening pages promise. The opening comes last, after every decision it must promise has been made.
