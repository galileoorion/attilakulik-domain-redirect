Start reading
  • From the beginningRead the chapters in order, as a course or a writing group would
  • With a full draftStart with The Structural Edit (Chapter 18), then the Symptom Index
  • With a single problemLook up what readers say in the Symptom Index and go to its chapter
  • With a planStart with Series and Genre (Chapter 17), in the order a book’s decisions depend on one another
