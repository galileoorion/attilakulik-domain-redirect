Chapter 1 · The Purposes of a Scene

10. The Scene Across Forms

Continue in Core

The domain model was developed for the novel. It transfers to adjacent forms with adjustments that need stating, because students frequently import novel-scene habits into forms that punish them.

10.1 The short story

The story’s characteristic difference is one of ratio: a scene may constitute a third of the whole.

The ratio leaves no room for normal-establishing scenes, so the normal must be established inside the scene that breaks it, usually by iterative clauses (clauses of habit, telling what used to happen every day) embedded in scenic narration.

10.2 The novella

The novella’s scene problem is that it is written by novelists. It has a novel’s tonal range and a fraction of its length, so a three-thousand-word scene consumes a twentieth of the book. The form’s characteristic solution is the compressed scene (mode 2): the occasion is rendered scenically but with aggressive internal ellipsis, so that the reader receives the texture of full dramatisation at half the cost. The Death of Ivan Ilyich is the model — Tolstoy dramatises perhaps a dozen occasions across a whole life, and summarises the rest without apology.

10.3 The screen comparison, and what does not transfer

Contemporary craft advice imports heavily from screenwriting — “enter late, leave early”, “every scene must turn”, “a scene occupies continuous time and space”. Each transfers to prose to a different degree.

Screen principle Transfers? Why
Enter late, leave early Yes The mechanism is identical: attention is finite and the audience reconstructs the approach without help
Every scene turns on a value Partly McKee’s formulation (§3.7) was written for feature-length film; he describes roughly forty to sixty scenes in a typical Archplot. A novel can also make room for scenes whose effect emerges across a larger span
A scene is a unit of continuous time and place Partly Screenwriting scenes are often organised around more or less continuous time and space, though a film scene may contain many cuts. Prose may also shift location, elide time or move between minds within a larger dramatic unit

One further asymmetry is worth teaching.

  • Film can summarise through montage, voice-over, transitions and ellipsis. These devices make compression differently perceptible from a prose sentence such as Three years passed. Prose can move into summary without a separate visual device; overlooking that option can produce the errand scene (§8.6).

10.4 Translated fiction

Structural claims survive translation. The counterpoint in Flaubert’s agricultural show, the double focalisation of Tolstoy’s steeplechase, the deviation from plan in Dostoevsky’s murder chapter and the interior progression of James’s chapter 42 are all present in every competent version, because they are properties of arrangement rather than of wording.

Concept Best displayed by Section
The value shift at maximum clarity Pride and Prejudice, the Hunsford proposal §5.2, §9.3
Multiplicity, organised density Madame Bovary, the agricultural show §9.2
