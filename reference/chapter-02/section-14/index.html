14.1 Criticism, narratology and theory

  • Aristotle. Poetics. c. 335 BCE. Available in Classical Literary Criticism, translated by Penelope Murray and T. S. Dorsch. London: Penguin, 2000.
  • Bakhtin, Mikhail. Problems of Dostoevsky’s Poetics. Translated by Caryl Emerson. Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 1984.
  • Barthes, Roland. S/Z. Translated by Richard Miller. New York: Hill and Wang, 1974. [French original 1970.]
  • Barthes, Roland. “Introduction to the Structural Analysis of Narratives.” In Image–Music–Text, translated by Stephen Heath. London: Fontana, 1977. [French original 1966.]
  • Brooks, Peter. Reading for the Plot: Design and Intention in Narrative. New York: Alfred A. Knopf, 1984.
  • Burke, Kenneth. Counter-Statement. New York: Harcourt, Brace, 1931.
  • Burke, Kenneth. A Grammar of Motives. New York: Prentice-Hall, 1945.
  • Chatman, Seymour. Story and Discourse: Narrative Structure in Fiction and Film. Ithaca: Cornell University Press, 1978.
  • Genette, Gérard. Narrative Discourse: An Essay in Method. Translated by Jane E. Lewin. Ithaca: Cornell University Press, 1980.
  • McGurl, Mark. The Program Era: Postwar Fiction and the Rise of Creative Writing. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 2009.
  • Propp, Vladimir. Morphology of the Folktale. Translated by Laurence Scott. 2nd ed. Austin: University of Texas Press, 1968. [Russian original 1928.]
  • Shklovsky, Viktor. “Art as Device.” 1917. In Theory of Prose, translated by Benjamin Sher. Elmwood Park, IL: Dalkey Archive, 1990.
  • Sternberg, Meir. Expositional Modes and Temporal Ordering in Fiction. Baltimore: Johns Hopkins University Press, 1978.
  • Todorov, Tzvetan. The Poetics of Prose. Translated by Richard Howard. Ithaca: Cornell University Press, 1977. [French original 1971.]

14.2 Cognitive and empirical studies of reading

  • Graesser, Arthur C., Murray Singer, and Tom Trabasso. “Constructing Inferences During Narrative Text Comprehension.” Psychological Review 101, no. 3 (1994): 371–395.
  • Trabasso, Tom, and Paul van den Broek. “Causal Thinking and the Representation of Narrative Events.” Journal of Memory and Language 24 (1985).
  • Zwaan, Rolf A., and Gabriel A. Radvansky. “Situation Models in Language Comprehension and Memory.” Psychological Bulletin 123, no. 2 (1998): 162–185.

14.3 Craft literature, used as documents of a practice

  • Bickham, Jack M. Scene & Structure. Cincinnati: Writer’s Digest Books, 1993.
  • Forster, E. M. Aspects of the Novel. London: Edward Arnold, 1927.
  • McKee, Robert. Story: Substance, Structure, Style and the Principles of Screenwriting. New York: ReganBooks, 1997.
  • Swain, Dwight V. Techniques of the Selling Writer. Norman: University of Oklahoma Press, 1965.
  • Yorke, John. Into the Woods: A Five-Act Journey into Story. London: Particular Books, 2013.
