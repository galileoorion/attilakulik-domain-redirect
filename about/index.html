The author

Attila Kulik is a writer based in Cambridge, UK. While revising his speculative fiction manuscript, he set out to understand why some passages resonated with beta readers and others did not. That research became The Craft Behind the Story, a practical guide for writers who know their manuscript needs work but aren’t sure what to change or where to begin. He is now applying its methods to his own manuscript.
